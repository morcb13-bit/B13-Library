What it does

The only operations this calculator applies to numbers are adding and comparing which is larger. Subtraction flips the sign and adds. Multiplication adds a to itself to build the ladder a, 2a, 4a, 8a, … and adds up the rungs that make b. Division subtracts rungs of the divisor's ladder from the top while they fit. Square root tries adding 1, 2, 4, … to the candidate and keeps each one whose square stays within the number.

π is 16·atan(1/5) − 4·atan(1/239), e is 1/0! + 1/1! + 1/2! + …, and φ is (1 + √5) ÷ 2, each computed as integers scaled by a power of 10 with the same addition tools. Decimals are truncated toward zero at the chosen digit, negative numbers included.

Balanced ternary uses the three digits −1, 0, +1 and balanced base 13 uses the thirteen digits −6 … +6 to write the same number shown above. Answers with decimals get a radix point in the balanced digits too. Blue digits are positive, red are negative.

The counts are the additions and comparisons needed for the answer shown (for division and √, down to the decimal digits). Converting to balanced digits is not counted.
